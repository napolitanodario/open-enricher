JC Trotter is a former rapper struggling with grief and financial difficulties. He works as a roofer alongside his boss, Joey, whose wife suffers from dementia. Facing mounting financial pressure, the two devise a fraudulent social media scheme involving a fictional online identity, hoping to raise enough money to establish a legitimate record label.

They enlist Ray Ray and Jabba, an unstable former convict, to help execute their plan. Although the operation initially generates substantial profits, the situation deteriorates when Jabba murders a rival at a nightclub and begins blackmailing his associates.

Ray Ray subsequently betrays the group and disappears with their money. The conflict escalates when Jabba tries to abduct JC's sister, but JC's grandmother intervenes and fatally shoots him.

Having lost their illegally obtained fortune, JC and Joey resume their roofing jobs. They encounter Travis, a construction worker with a remarkable talent for country music. Abandoning their criminal methods, they establish SOB Records by pursuing genuine musical talent, finding a new opportunity for redemption.
