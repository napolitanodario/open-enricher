Ifedayo and Ifeoluwa are identical mirror twins raised by their mother. Although physically almost indistinguishable, they develop contrasting personalities and habits. Ifedayo is reserved, disciplined, academically successful, and left-handed, while Ifeoluwa is outspoken, impulsive, protective, and right-handed. Ifeoluwa possesses an exceptional photographic memory and dreams of becoming a lawyer, but ultimately studies Theatre Arts, while Ifedayo pursues a law degree.

Following difficulties at university and growing tensions within the family, Ifeoluwa moves to the United Kingdom, hoping to establish herself as a social media influencer. Her ambitions fail to materialize, and she ends up working in a bar.

Twelve years later, Ifedayo has become a successful lawyer at Apex Legal and is approaching a partnership. Among her professional responsibilities is a divorce case involving Kola Adeyemi and his wife, Michelle.

Ifeoluwa returns to Nigeria and reunites with her sister after years of separation. She persuades Ifedayo to accompany her on a night out involving drinking and dancing. When they prepare to leave, Ifedayo expresses concerns about driving under the influence. Ifeoluwa nevertheless insists on driving them home. A subsequent car accident kills Ifedayo, leaving Ifeoluwa overwhelmed by guilt.

Their devastated mother stands before Ifedayo's portrait and expresses disbelief that she was the daughter who died. Ifeoluwa overhears her mother's remarks, which reinforce her feelings of inadequacy and the longstanding comparisons between the sisters.

Ifeoluwa accesses Ifedayo's apartment using her passcode. While attempting to exchange a hundred-dollar bill on the street, she becomes involved in a dispute with a money changer. A colleague recognizes her as Ifedayo, intervenes, and informs her that the firm has been expecting her return.

Ifeoluwa assumes her deceased sister's identity and enters Apex Legal, intending to continue Ifedayo's career and secure the partnership.

Her unfamiliarity with legal practice and her different habits threaten to expose the deception. Ifedayo's close friend Iheoma notices that the woman claiming to be her friend uses her right hand, prefers coffee with creamer instead of drinking it black, and places her handbag differently in the car.

Iheoma confronts Ifeoluwa and initially demands that she abandon both the apartment and the firm. However, she subsequently changes her mind and encourages Ifeoluwa to stop abandoning difficult situations. She begins helping her prepare for her professional responsibilities.

The divorce case becomes a murder investigation when Michelle Adeyemi is poisoned. Her husband, Kola, becomes the primary suspect. His fingerprints are reportedly found on the drinking glass associated with her death, and an earlier threatening remark strengthens suspicions against him.

Despite lacking formal legal qualifications, Ifeoluwa studies the case documents intensively, relying on her photographic memory and Iheoma's assistance. After rehearsing her courtroom arguments with Iheoma, she successfully argues that Kola is unlikely to flee, supporting his application for bail.

Meanwhile, Ifeoluwa develops a romantic connection with Femi Adeyemi, a colleague and Kola's cousin. She also encounters Mr. Awade, who had secretly admired Ifedayo. Unlike Femi, Mr. Awade eventually recognizes that the woman is not Ifedayo. He confronts Ifeoluwa but chooses not to disclose her true identity.

As the murder investigation progresses, Ifeoluwa notices discrepancies in the evidence. Her photographic memory allows her to recognize that an orange mobile phone is missing from the crime scene following Michelle's death.

Another breakthrough comes through Chimamanda, whose school project involves identifying digitally manipulated images. Inconsistencies in the lighting of a photograph help expose evidence that has been artificially altered.

During the courtroom proceedings, Ifeoluwa challenges the authenticity of the property evidence and uncovers a secret relationship between Michelle and Steve, who were involved in misappropriating money.

The investigation ultimately reveals Steve as Michelle's murderer. The evidence used to implicate Kola is exposed as part of an attempt to frame him. The circumstances surrounding the drinking glass, including its handling and the use of a tissue, become relevant to the reconstruction of the crime.

Ifeoluwa successfully concludes the case and secures the partnership at Apex Legal. Her professional success also changes some of her relationships within the firm. Tonia, previously antagonistic toward her, eventually initiates a lunch invitation.

Although only a small number of people know her true identity, Ifeoluwa gradually moves beyond the guilt and sense of failure that have defined her life. Rather than continuing to rely entirely on her sister's identity and qualifications, she eventually enrolls in law school, taking steps toward fulfilling her original ambition of becoming a lawyer herself.
