Matt (Pauly Shore) and Erwin (Nick Swardson) work the overnight shift at Highway 50 Gas and Liquor, an isolated convenience store in the desert.

To relieve their boredom, Matt attempts to engage Erwin in conversation, asking about his favorite song from the 2000s. Erwin chooses Imogen Heap's "Hide and Seek" but prefers scrolling through TikTok. They also debate instant gratification and processed snacks. Erwin refuses Matt's chips because he is following the Whole30 diet.

An unusually tall customer enters the restroom. The employees mention that its toilet seat does not stay upright.

A formally dressed stranger known as the Grin Man (Doug Jones) arrives with an exaggerated smile. Identifying himself as a federal government employee, he explains that a dangerous creature named Mr. Frett (Kevin Keppy) has escaped from a research facility in the desert. It detects stress through human body language and devours frightened individuals.

The agent instructs both employees to remain cheerful, requests reinforcements, and investigates the restroom carrying a syringe. Finding discarded clothes and an open ventilation duct, he looks inside and discovers the creature. He initially maintains his composure, but the toilet seat suddenly falls, startling him and triggering an attack.

Hearing screams, Matt and Erwin investigate. They find the agent suspended above the sink before he is violently pulled into the ventilation system. The employees flee through the store as the creature pursues them through the ceiling.

Remembering the agent's warning, Matt forces Erwin to smile and attempts to comfort him by singing the song mentioned earlier, despite not knowing it.

They decide to drink beer to reduce their anxiety before attempting to reach their car. Matt approaches the refrigerator while Erwin monitors the ceiling vent.

When Matt removes a beer can, he discovers Mr. Frett concealed behind the drinks. He retreats as the creature emerges from the refrigerator. Meanwhile, the agent's severed head falls through the vent near Erwin.

Mr. Frett approaches Matt, who unsuccessfully attempts to suppress his panic. The creature attacks and kills him, splattering Erwin's face with blood.

Now alone, Erwin abandons his dietary restrictions and distracts himself with snacks and TikTok videos. These activities allow him to endure the remainder of the night.

The following morning, two government agents arrive and instruct Erwin to continue watching his phone while they attempt to rescue him. However, his phone runs out of battery. Without his distraction, Erwin loses his composure as Mr. Frett appears behind him.

The film cuts to its title card accompanied by screams and sounds of an attack, strongly implying Erwin's death without explicitly showing it.

In a mid-credits scene, the two government agents stand in the doorway, laughing and acknowledging that they will probably lose their jobs. The creature's fate remains unresolved.
